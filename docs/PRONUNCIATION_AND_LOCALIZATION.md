**Prayer:** The text at [8.3.2](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/8/3/2/) includes `oṁ namo bhagavate vāsudevāya`. Do **not** silently substitute a different mantra and represent it as that verse. If an alternate traditional refrain is chosen, document it clearly as an editorial selection and confirm native pronunciation.

**Speech style:** simple modern Telugu first, natural contemporary Hindi second, accessible English third; respect proper devotional address. The elephant and crocodile normally express themselves through action, ambient sound and internal/spiritual narration, not moving lips as if they speak like people. Sanskrit chanting and spoken paraphrase have different tracks and rights/provenance.

Each future line ID needs separate native-language listening notes and approval; the presence of a Unicode script alone is not a language check.
