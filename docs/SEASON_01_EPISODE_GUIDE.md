## Editorial pacing notes

- **S01E01:** Establish the beauty and herd before danger; the cliffhanger is the crocodile's grip, not a fabricated villain speech.
- **S01E02:** Use contrast between water and land, the herd's efforts and a long-time montage. End with Gajendra's inward turn.
- **S01E03:** Give prayer enough room. Meaningful verse selection and original child-friendly paraphrase need human theological and linguistic review.
- **S01E04:** Keep the rescue swift; scripture names the disc and the crocodile's death, but do not show blood.
- **S01E05:** Clearly mark flashbacks. Hūhū's incident and Indradyumna's vow are separate; neither implies either sage sought Gajendra's death.
- **S01E06:** Complete the theological ending: Hūhū restored, Gajendra's four-armed liberation, benediction and departure with the Lord. Do not end with a rescued elephant merely returning to his herd.
