# Rights, references and responsible publication

Project-owned original drafts, character design briefs and production metadata should be reviewed for rights before distribution; no license has been granted by inclusion in this public repository. All named mythological characters and scriptural narratives are traditional; that does **not** grant reuse rights to any modern publisher's commentary, translation, recorded chanting, photographs, generated artwork, music, voices or film footage.

Scriptural reference links in `docs/SOURCE_REGISTER.md` are research citations only. Draft fresh Telugu/Hindi/English spoken text rather than copying substantial modern translations verbatim. Record music, voice and image source/approval/provenance in the asset registry. Obtain rights or permissions before using third-party media. Do not publish unreleased personal voice samples or API credentials.
